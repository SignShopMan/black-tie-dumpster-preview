Frequently Asked Questions

What can I throw in the dumpster?+

Most household and construction debris is fine — furniture, appliances, yard waste, shingles, drywall, and general junk. Hazardous materials like paint, tires, batteries, and chemicals are not accepted.

How long do I get to keep the dumpster?+

Standard rentals include a 7 day rental period. Need more time? Extended rental periods are available — just give us a call.

What if I go over the weight limit?+

Overage is charged per ton above the included weight (2 tons for the 12 yard, 3 tons for the 20 yard). We'll let you know before pickup if your load looks like it's over.

Do I need a permit for the dumpster?+

If the dumpster is placed entirely on your private property (driveway), no permit is needed. If it needs to go on a public street or sidewalk, a permit may be required — we can help you figure that out.
